# Editorial Guidelines

## Fact Versus Analysis

Separate documented findings from analysis. Do not imply direct coordination where the evidence only shows ideological similarity, repeated language, or shared narrative framing.

Aggregate findings must be labelled as aggregate. Do not convert totals into individual-level claims unless row-level source data has been verified and cleared for publication.

Ideological classifications describe the analysed sample. They do not establish that every account belonged to, was directed by, or formally represented named organisations. The Al-Qaeda category includes overlapping TTP ties in the source totals; researchers must clarify whether categories are mutually exclusive and how overlap was coded before final publication.

Identity classifications reflect researcher assessment of publicly observable profile information. "Apparently real names and photographs" must not be written as independently verified legal identity.

## Attribution

Attribute the report to Hasan Al Mahmud and preserve the report date. Claims added to the interactive edition require source review before publication.

## Extremist Excerpts

Use short excerpts only when necessary for reader understanding. Do not reproduce long extremist statements. Add content warnings before sections containing incitement or violent rhetoric.

## Redaction

Use anonymised labels such as Account A. Remove direct handles, profile URLs, emails, phone numbers, addresses, family information, and private researcher notes from public output.

## Corrections

Maintain a corrections process that records what changed, when it changed, and why. Corrections should distinguish factual errors from clarifications.

## Right of Reply

Any future publication workflow should include an editorial decision on right-of-reply outreach, timing, and safety considerations.

## Avoiding Amplification

Do not link to extremist accounts or websites. Avoid sensational framing, extremist symbols, decorative weapons, and language that turns propaganda into promotional material.

## Scrollytelling Standards

Scrollytelling must not exaggerate evidence. Visual connections, routes, repeated markers, and proportional fields must reflect verified data or be explicitly labelled as conceptual or diagrammatic.

Reconstructions must be labelled as reconstructions and must not imitate a real platform interface closely enough to look like fabricated evidence.

Redacted excerpts and term markers must preserve enough context for interpretation without reproducing harmful text unnecessarily.

Motion should clarify sequence and emphasis. It must not sensationalise violent rhetoric, hostile propaganda, grief, or public tragedy.

## Evidence Publication

Never mark an evidence item approved until an editor has authorised that specific derivative. Public evidence must not contain direct account URLs, unredacted names, unrelated commenters, private archive references, or long extremist passages.

For sensitive violent-incitement material, readers must be able to continue without revealing the evidence. Warnings should be factual and neutral, not guilt-inducing.

In the review studio, "review configuration complete" means only that the internal redaction/caption/status plan is complete. It does not authorise publication.

Evidence release requires per-item newsroom sign-off after mapping, redaction, caption, accessibility, legal, privacy, translation, archive, right-of-reply, content warning, and annotation approval checks are complete.
